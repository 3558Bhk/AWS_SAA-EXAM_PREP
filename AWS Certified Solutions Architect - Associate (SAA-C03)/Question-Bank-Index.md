## How to use this bank

1. Study the task's `notes.md` first, then attempt its `practicequestions.md`.
2. Work **Section A straight through** - these are the single-answer questions and they follow real-exam topic priority, so the first questions in every file are the ones most likely to appear.
3. Then work **Section B**. Multiple-response questions are where most candidates lose marks: the question always states how many to select, and there is no partial credit.
4. Check the **Answer Key & Explanations** at the bottom of each file. Where the source provided them, you also get a *Why not X* note for each distractor.
5. Finish with `Full-Length-Mock-Exam.md` under timed conditions, then revise `Exam-Day-Cheatsheet.md`.
